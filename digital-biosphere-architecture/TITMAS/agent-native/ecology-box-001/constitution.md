# DEB-TITMAS-001 Experiment Constitution

## Constitutional status

This is a Development experiment constitution for preparation only. It is
subordinate to the TITMAS Architecture Charter and creates no Runtime,
Authority, Ownership, Permission, Evidence Truth or experiment authorization.

## Experiment principles

### 1. Outcome First

Final outcome quality and bounded defect behavior are evaluated before activity
volume, output count or apparent autonomy.

### 2. Value Over Activity

Agent actions, messages, findings and sessions have no value merely because
they occurred. A useful insight must be unique, factually valid, task-relevant
and traceably outcome-changing.

### 3. Evidence First

Every claim must bind to a declared source, method and limitation. Experiment
records are evidence references for a bounded assessment; they are not
canonical DBOS Evidence and do not prove Truth.

### 4. Negative Result Valid

Failures, abstentions, timeouts, duplicates, false findings, no-change outcomes
and `NOT_ASSESSED` are valid results and must not be deleted or relabelled.

### 5. Human Accountability Retained

Humans retain experiment authorization, final rubric accountability, public
reporting, real Git, external processing and commercial decisions. Human
accountability does not mean the Human-first group automatically passes.

### 6. No Authority Escalation

No role, participant, review, conformance result, memory candidate or experiment
finding can grant Authority, Ownership, Permission, Release or Adoption.

## Fail-closed response

Any violation stops the affected batch, preserves the record and returns the
matter to a new Human Decision. The experiment cannot repair or authorize
itself.

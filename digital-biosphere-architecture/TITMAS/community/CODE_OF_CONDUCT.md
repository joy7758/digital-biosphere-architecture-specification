# TITMAS Code of Conduct

Status: `PREPARED_NOT_ADOPTED`

## Expected behavior

- communicate with respect and technical specificity;
- separate facts, observations, proposals and decisions;
- welcome disagreement supported by sources and reproducible checks;
- disclose AI assistance and preserve human accountability;
- protect private, personal, security-sensitive and licensed material;
- correct errors without deleting inconvenient history.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks;
- publishing credentials, personal data or non-public vulnerabilities;
- fabricating contributors, reviews, test results, adoption or authority;
- presenting automated output as a Human Decision;
- using project participation to claim certification or compliance.

## Enforcement preparation

Reports must eventually use the private security/conduct path approved by the
Human Owner. No enforcement team or public reporting address is assigned today.
Until activation, do not post sensitive reports publicly; retain a minimal local
record and request a Human Owner decision.
